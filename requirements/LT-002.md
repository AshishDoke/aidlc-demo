# LT-002 Ask for leave and decide on it

## Who
Employees who want time off, and the managers who approve it.

## Problem
Now that the system knows our people (LT-001), it needs to support the actual process:
an employee asks, the manager decides. The screens come later (LT-003, LT-004), and they will rely on this.

## What we want
- Anyone can see the list of employees, so they can pick who they are.
- An employee can ask for leave for themselves. The request starts as pending.
- An employee can see all their own requests, newest first.
- A manager can see the pending requests of the people in their team, and only their team.
- A manager can approve or reject a pending request from someone in their team.
  A rejection must include a reason. An approval can include an optional comment.
- Once a request has been approved or rejected, it cannot be decided again.
- Anyone can look up a single request.
- Every request shows how many days it covers.
- When something is not allowed or doesn't make sense, the person gets a clear message saying why.
  For example: the request doesn't exist, it's not your team, it was already decided,
  or the last day is before the first day.

## Not included
- The detailed rules (past dates, maximum length, overlaps, weekends). Those come in LT-005.
- Logging in. For now people pick who they are.

## Still to decide
- Can an employee cancel their own request while it is still pending?
