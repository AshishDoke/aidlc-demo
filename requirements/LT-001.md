# LT-001 Keep track of employees and their leave

## Who
HR, managers and employees of a small team.

## Problem
Leave is tracked in spreadsheets and emails. Nobody is sure who is off when, or whether a request was approved.
Before we build any screens, the system needs to know about our people and their leave.

## What we want
- The system knows our employees: their name and email, and who is a manager.
  Everyone who is not a manager has exactly one manager.
- No two employees can have the same email.
- The system can hold leave requests. Each one records who asked, the first and last day,
  the type of leave (annual, sick or unpaid) and an optional reason.
- A new request is pending. Later the manager approves or rejects it, and we keep who decided,
  when, and any comment they left.
- For demos and testing there is a ready-made team: one manager, three people reporting to them,
  and a few example requests in different states. Restarting the app must not create the team twice.

## Not included
- Any screens.
- Asking for or deciding on leave. That comes in LT-002.
