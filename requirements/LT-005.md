# LT-005 Leave rules from HR

## Who
HR, on behalf of all employees and managers.

## Problem
The basic process works, but people can currently ask for leave that makes no sense.
HR wants these rules applied to every request.

## Rules
1. You can't ask for leave in the past. Sick leave is the exception: it can be entered up to 7 days after the fact.
2. The last day can't be before the first day.
3. A single request can't be longer than 10 days.
4. You can't have two requests that overlap, unless the earlier one was rejected.
5. A request that falls only on a weekend makes no sense and isn't allowed.
6. The reason is optional, but it can't be longer than 500 characters.

## What we want
- Every rule is checked whenever someone asks for leave.
- When a rule is broken, the person sees a clear message saying which rule it is, on the employee screen next to the form.

## Examples
- Exactly 10 days is allowed. 11 is not.
- Two requests that share just one day count as overlapping.
- Sick leave from 3 days ago is fine. Sick leave from 8 days ago is not.

## Not included
- Public holidays.
